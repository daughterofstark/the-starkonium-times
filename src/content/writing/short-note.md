longer essay, but it should not sprout a table of contents, a progress bar, a
related-reading footer, or any other theatrical machinery. A short public note
needs a title, a date, a clear standfirst, topics, and enough body text to prove
that the prose system is active.

paragraph rhythm, link styling through an inline [placeholder link](/writing),
and the absence of visual demotion. The content is fake on purpose: no real
biography, no imported Blogger post, and no research claim hiding inside test
